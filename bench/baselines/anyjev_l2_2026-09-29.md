    .venv/bin/python $MESA_CLM/scripts/anyjev_l2_predictions.py \
        --provenance duckdb:///$PWD/.local/labels.duckdb --out <file>

The script is mesa-anyjev's `bench.run._fit_eval_loco` at level `L2` for `neon_term_fits` and
`neon_ontology_fits`, step for step, with every held-out `DecisionRecord` kept instead of only
pooled: labels at the bench `min_weight` 0.5 (highest weight per state), each card held out in
sorted order, the 30/5 fold guards, a fresh fitting Decider per fold (`fit_on(..., "L2")`: the
block and the head kind chosen by out-of-fold NLL, `n_folds=5`, `seed=0`), the held-out states
decided at L2.

## How K2(a) pairs against it (DESIGN K2, X2)

K2(a) needs mesa-clm's best servable tier to be non-inferior to AnyJev L2 *paired* on the full
set: the one-sided cluster lower bound of Δacc (mesa-clm − AnyJev) above −0.02 with the
card-sign condition of rule R, besides `beats_lookup_novel` and ECE ≤ 0.08 with cluster upper
bound ≤ 0.12 for mesa-clm's own cell. The pairing:

- **Items.** Each item carries its `card` (= `fold`), `state_sha256`, the stored `state_json`, the
  compact `target` (dataset, scope, column or site, aspect, option key), the `label` (0 Yes, 1
  No) and `p_yes`. mesa-clm derives the D1 identity `(task_key, target_sha256, option_key)` from
  `state_json` at import (`mesa_clm.identity`), never in the dump script. Checked on 2026-10-01:
  all 285 term.fits and 190 column.ontology_fits items map to distinct identities, every one
  pairs with a row of mesa-clm's frozen snapshot `bench/snapshots/2026-09-29.parquet`
  (`labels_sha256` `aafd18f8…752c`), and the label and the card agree on every pair.
- **Folds.** Both sides hold out the same seven cards in sorted order with the same 30/5 guards,
  so a pair is held out by the same card on both sides; mesa-clm's cell uses its own nested
  selection inside each fold (D27), AnyJev L2 its out-of-fold selection above.
- **Statistic.** Per held-out card, Δacc over the paired items; the card-cluster paired bootstrap
  of rule R (B = 2000, seed 0) gives the one-sided 95% lower bound, and the card-sign condition
  counts the cards with ≥ 10 evaluable items. An item without a partner (a label set that moved)
  is reported with its identity, never silently dropped; a moved label set means a new snapshot,
  hence a new pairing.
- **Metrics.** Accuracy needs no ordering, so the vendored and the tie-invariant metrics agree on
  it (D33); ECE and the coverage numbers above are mesa-anyjev's own and are quoted, not re-cited:
  the K2(a) cell recomputes everything it gates on from the per-item `p_yes` with
  `mesa_clm.bench.metrics`.
